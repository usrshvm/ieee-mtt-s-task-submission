What is IEEE MTT-S?

The IEEE Microwave Theory and Techniques Society (MTT-S) is a technical society of IEEE for engineers and students working in microwave and RF technology, from theory and components to systems and applications.

Its work supports everyday technology: mobile networks, satellite links, radar, Wi-Fi and the sensors behind modern electronics.

Visit the official MTT-S website

About the chapter

The MITS-DU Gwalior chapter gives students a place to learn RF and microwave engineering beyond the syllabus. We run talks, hands-on sessions and small projects, and connect members with the wider IEEE community.

Whether you are a first-year student or about to graduate, you are welcome to join, ask questions and build something with us.
